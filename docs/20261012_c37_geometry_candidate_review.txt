2. Same native images, same v3 weights, input sizes 640 / 768 / 960
   616 saved images had exact image-time camera pose records. Each image was
   inferred at all three sizes; ground-ray errors were evaluated offline only.
   White frames with at least one full-foot position within 1 m: 150/152/157.
   Compared with 640, both larger sizes lost 21 previously good white frames;
   they gained 23/28 other white frames. Thus the gain is not monotonic.
   Blue geometric errors >=1 m: 45/41/57 (including distant false identities).
   Green good frames: 40/40/40. Red good frames: 210/220/227.
   CPU median inference: 12.3/14.3/19.0 ms in this offline process, not a
   six-aircraft latency measurement. Archival ran concurrently.
   This is not full-pipeline recall or a competition result: samples were
   event-triggered, tracking/appearance filters were not replayed, and the
   frame-level good-point count does not include every formal reporting gate.
   Evidence: artifacts/c37_geometry_followup_20261012/image_size_comparison.json
